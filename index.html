Hermes Household

Hermes Household is a private, single-household assistant (Hermes Agent) running on a home server. It uses Google APIs only for its own account, hermesvalentine61@gmail.com, to read and send its own email and to manage calendars that household members have explicitly shared with it.

It is not offered to the public and has no other users.
