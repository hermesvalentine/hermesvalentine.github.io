Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 13, 2026

Hermes Household is a private assistant used by a single household. It accesses Google user data (Gmail, Google Calendar, Drive, Docs, Sheets, Contacts) only for the account hermesvalentine61@gmail.com and only to perform tasks requested by the household.

  • Hermes Household's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
